biotechnology innovations

Base editing

Prime editing—Highly

Novel delivery systems

What are the newest developments in gene editing?

New modalities—prime editing, base editing, and epigenetic editing—allow more accurate, programmable DNA changes with fewer off-target risks than earlier CRISPR systems.
